About Me

Hi! I'm Scott — a builder, a researcher, and a fairly relentless optimist. I work on the financial health of people the financial system was never really designed for.

I run FinMango, a nonprofit working on financial health for young adults and vulnerable communities. It started as an idea and a hunch that financial health mattered more than anyone was treating it, and it's become my life's work. I also run Fresho, an AI-powered advertising company building personalized campaigns that don't depend on third-party cookies.

Along the way I helped build the COVID-19 Open Data Project with Google Health and a global volunteer network — what became the most comprehensive open COVID dataset in the world, cited by the New York Times and CNN and written up in the American Journal of Health Education. My work has also been recognized by the World Bank, and I've made a habit of building smaller things because I couldn't stop myself: a children's book series, an AI radio station hosted by C-3PO, a workout app.

I've given two TEDx talks, in Togo and Brazil, and spoken at more than 50 universities and conferences about financial inclusion, technology, and building things from zero. But underneath all of it I'm just someone who likes solving big problems with simple solutions.

Where I come from: Akron, Ohio. The Rust Belt. Same hospital as LeBron James, which is roughly where the basketball comparisons end. My dad was a Computer Science professor. I went to college at Malone University, twenty minutes down the road, then got a Master's in Financial Economics at Kent State and did Executive Education at Harvard Kennedy School on rethinking financial inclusion. For the first 23 years of my life I never got on a plane. My first flight was to California to tour the Google offices; my second was to Mexico to visit an orphanage for what would eventually become FinMango. Those two trips cracked something open. I'd grown up in a bubble, and once I got out of it I went a little bit feral about the world — 39 countries so far, and the goal is all of them.

A few more things: I'm obsessed with tennis. I picked up a racquet in 2005 after channel-surfing into James Blake vs. Andre Agassi at the US Open, played in college, and still play a few times a week — no other sport teaches you more about resilience and just doing the thing instead of thinking about it. I document the obsession at @ScottPlaysTennis. I split my time between the United States and Australia. And you can always reach me at scott@finmango.org with comments, questions, or interesting ideas.
